JARVIS FOR YOUR PHONE  (no computer needed)

WHAT YOU GET
An installable app that runs entirely on your phone: voice, AI brain, notes, to-do list, timers, weather,
calculator, "drop my needle", clap-to-wake. Nothing runs on a computer.

PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE (about 10 minutes, all from the phone)
1. Get a free AI key: open console.groq.com/keys in your phone browser, sign in, create a key, copy it.
2. Host the files (free): open github.com, sign in, tap New repository, name it jarvis, set Public, Create.
   Tap "uploading an existing file", pick these 7 files (not the zip), tap Commit changes:
     index.html  manifest.webmanifest  sw.js  icon-192.png  icon-512.png  icon-maskable-512.png  apple-touch-icon.png
3. In the repository tap Settings, then Pages. Under Source choose "Deploy from a branch", branch main, folder /root, Save.
   After a minute or two it shows your link: https://YOURNAME.github.io/jarvis/
4. Open that link.
   Android (Chrome): menu with three dots, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
   iPhone (Safari): Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
5. Open Jarvis from your home screen, tap Start, allow the microphone, open Settings and paste your key. Done.

YOUR KEY STAYS ON THE PHONE. It is never inside any of these files, so the public page contains no secrets.

USING HIM
- Say "Jarvis" then your request, or type. Tap the mic to turn listening on or off.
- "Jarvis, drop my needle" shows a Play button for your song (browsers will not start YouTube by themselves).
- "go to sleep" or 3 idle minutes puts him to sleep. Clap twice to wake him.
- Notes: say "remember that ..." They appear as holograms in the ring. Export/Import in Settings backs them up.

HONEST LIMITS OF A PHONE BROWSER APP
- Best on Android with Chrome. iPhone voice recognition is limited and may not work; typing always works.
- He only hears you while the app is open with the screen on (he keeps the screen awake for you).
- Clap-to-wake works only while he is asleep, because a phone cannot do clap-listening and speech recognition at once.
- Timers only ring while the app is open.
- Not included from the computer version: Workshop Buddy guides, 3D printer control, camera/posture vision,
  controlling the computer, and live web search (he answers from knowledge, plus weather via open-meteo).
